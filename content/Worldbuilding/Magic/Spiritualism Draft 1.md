A person exists within five parts:
- The Body is what you physically are, the nerves, the flesh, the blood.
- The Mind is how you model reality, it is what gives you your ability to logic, understand, and learn
- The Spirit is what you feel and resonate with, it is your emotion, instinct, and drives
- The Soul is what connects and defines you, it is your identity across time, your relationships, and your alignment to meaning and roles
- The Senses are what you perceive as real, not just sight and hearing but your perspective-- how you interpret the world

Any can exist without the other, but without all five you are not truly a person.
You can have a body but no mind, a mind but no soul, a spirit but no senses, or any other combination. All of these parts exist separately, but are not fully utilized unless all five combine. This is the foundation of spiritualism that all five magical traditions are established on.

Without...
The body, you cannot interact with the world
The mind, you cannot form complex beliefs
The spirit, you cannot feel emotions or relate to others
The soul, you cannot form relationships or hold an identity
The senses, you cannot perceive the world

#### Arcane
> **"TO KNOW IS TO SHAPE"**

To cast an arcane spell you must first construct a mental theorem about reality. If your logic is internally consistent then reality complies. If it is flawed or forced, then reality rejects you and you experience backlash.

Instead of gestures, words, or mana, they "derive" spells by creating thought patterns, axioms, and philosophies. Deriving a spell will always fatigue the mind, but powerful, expensive spells might fundamentally alter how you think. Powerful spells could lead to the loss of intuition, inability to understand or relate to normal people, multiple personalities, and the "crystallization of thought". You are not spending energy, you are replacing your mind with something more precise but less human.

Most arcane casters will generally try to "bias" their cognition to affect their spells. Your philosophy on how you see the world fundamentally alters how you cast spells. Realistic, grounded philosophies might find it difficult to work with abstract concepts, but easier to cast tangible spells with physical results. Nihilistic or negative philosophies might find spells with altruistic or constructive effects harder to cast, but be better at hurting others or deconstruction. There is no wrong philosophy, however, a flawed understanding of a philosophy might impair your ability to cast.

Arcane spells are about reinterpreting things around you, defining barriers, and collapsing probability into certainty.
Ways spells can go wrong:
- If your theorem is inconsistent, the spell fizzles out.
- If your theorem is contradictory, you receive equivalent backlash.
- If you create a logical paradox then reality misfires and warps. If your logic is recursive, your mind may fracture
- A total collapse of your axioms might result in a permanent cognitive mutation
It should feel astronomical, abstract, "geometric", and maybe even a little wrong.

**Philosophical Inspirations:**
- Plato - Reality as abstract forms, axioms form reality
- Aristotle - Categorization, logic, causality, derivation
- Pythagoras - reality as numbers and harmony
- Descartes - I think, therefore I am
- Spinoza - Reality as a single unified system

**Readings:**

- Ethics by Baruch Spinoza

#### Primal
> **"TO BECOME IS TO COMMAND"**

To control a spirit you must align your identity with them. Power comes from resonating with a concept at a fundamental, emotional, and instinctual level.

To cast, you must align your emotions with that of a spirit, the stronger the alignment the stronger the effect. Primal casters tend to have many different exercises for getting into the right emotional state, like breathing exercises, mindfulness, environmental immersion, etc.

Casting any primal spell has a cost, you become more like the thing you cast. The more frequently you use one spirit the easier it is to channel it, but the harder it is to channel unrelated ones. Fire channelers might become more hotheaded and unstable, earth channelers might become more stubborn and inflexible, etc. There is not any one way to become like any one element, one fire may be impatient and explosive, another might be slow and calm. Casting in an aligned environment is to your benefit, as the spirit of that environment self is aiding you, with the opposite being true in opposing environments.

At extreme levels of familiarity, your personality may be completely usurped by that of your chosen spirit, and it will begin to claim your body as well. A beginner will start by imitating traits of their spirit, and a lucky few may already be emotionally suited to their chosen spirit. An intermediate caster will start separating their identity. An advanced caster has welcomed the emotions of their chosen spirit, temporarily invoking it, and a master will have fully merged, body and mind.

Primal spells should feel visceral, fluid, and embodied. You do not cast a fireball, you ARE fire, and the fire expands. You become the wind, and the wind is never hit. You let your anger manifest into lightning. Eventually, you become so "one" with an element that just that element no longer feels right to you and you have to become more specific. You are not just water, but a raging tsunami. You are not just lightning, but a battery ready to explode at a moment's notice, etc.

- Shinto? - Spirits tied to natural things
- Zen Buddhism - Direct experience over intellectualization

#### Divine
> **"TO BIND IS TO SHAPE REALITY"**

To understand the Divine, you must understand souls. Souls connect the four physical aspects, the Body, the Mind, the Spirit, and the Senses. Any sentient being with volition has a soul. All souls are connected by their relationships, the closer the relationship the closer and stronger the connection. Souls are like sponges for concepts and ideals, the stronger the conviction the stronger the soul.

Different bonds are structured differently:
- Mutual bonds are strong and stable
- Hierarchical bonds are strong, but can be exploited
- Ideological bonds are weaker but plentiful
- Broken bonds are dangerous and unstable

A king has power because people recognize them as a king, a priest has power because the followers believe they do, and a parent can only mold a child because they both believe so.

To cast a spell with divine magic, you must first anchor your soul to its relevant concepts, the route power from those connections. By anchoring your souls to these concepts you invite their influence. Divine magic is fundamentally, the authority to declare *what* something is.

Spells are declarations of meaning, enforcements of roles, and manipulation of bonds.
Examples of spells:
- "You are the aggressor" => They are now subject to punishment
- "I am your protector" => Gain power to defend
- "This place is sacred" => Restrain what behavior is allowed within an area
- "We are allies" => Share strength between comrades

Divine magic cannot create raw elements, rewrite physics, or manipulate flesh, but it can enforce and manipulate roles, relationships, and meaning. You cannot say "you have wronged me" unless it is true within your beliefs. You can't declare someone an ally without mutual or hierarchical structure.

Failures:
- Misaligned concept => Weak or inverted effect
- False role => You take the role instead
- Overextension => Relationships collapse under the strain
- Total dogma => You become an avatar of the concept

**Philosophical Inspirations:**

- Kabbalah - Interconnected souls
- Confucius - Relationships define reality
- Stoicism - Live according to your principles

**Readings:**

- The Republic by Plato
#### Occult
> **"POWER DEMANDS SOMETHING REAL"**

Occult magic is not unknowable, even if it is a bit chaotic. The occult is all about physical transaction, I offer this to you, I get x of yours in exchange. Now, generally such exchanges are of a more financial nature, however, with occult magic this is generally taken literally. Occult magic is all about codified agreements that anyone can take part in. However, messing up any part of the exchange can lead to disastrous consequences-- or worse, putting you in the debt of the other party.

All Occult magic has 3 parts:
- **Structure** - How the exchange must take place, the timing, location, and events leading up to the exchange
- **Payment** - What the initiator offers
- **Target** - What the initiator is trading to

All Occult magic has some irreversible cost. A lot of cheap spells may require a simple tax of blood for a meager effect, however many are a lot more expensive. A few examples:
- Give up your ability to receive pain, receive great resilience
- Give up your name, become impossible to track but lose your identity
- Give up your future children, gain immediate power
- Give up your memories, receive knowledge

The wording of a "contract" could be very opaque and non-specific. What does "Giving up the ability to feel pain" entail? It might not just mean offering up the sensation, but the physical nerve endings themselves. And even if it is just losing your sense of pain, that would mean *all* pain. You'd be completely unable to tell if you were being harmed.

The price and the reward are always specific, but may not be known to the initiator. Though, a lack of knowledge from one party only leaves them open to the debt of the other, where they could ask or take anything they view as a worthy trade for the time and resources wasted.

Philosophical Inspirations:
- Hermeticism - As above, so below
- Alchemy - Transformation through sacrifice, equivalent exchange
- Lovecraftian Mythos - The tone, unknowable but consistent

Readings:
- The Lesser Key of Solomon
- The Kybalion
#### Mystic
> **"PERSONAL TRUTH IS REALITY"**

There is no singular objective reality, and mysticism is all about exploiting that. There are no absolute truths or absolute laws, the only things that exist to you are what you can perceive and understand, and mystic magic is all about enforcing your truth onto the world. All mystics strive for religious ecstasy.

Casting a mystic spell means asserting a personal truth. It isn't an alignment, imitation, or manipulation, it's an insistence. However, just because something is true for one person doesn't mean it's true for everyone. Reality itself, as well as everyone around you, have their own truths. And if your assertion isn't forceful enough to overcome their own, or not complete enough to actualize, your truth will be rejected. The less an area is perceived, the more it is susceptible to someone's truth. A crowded city with hundreds of thousands of people would be nearly impossible to change because everyone there perceives it the same way, and its hundreds of thousands of truths against one. But a secluded area in the forest with no animals would be trivial to assert truths in.

All magic has a cost, and while mysticism is powerful it is still subject to rules. The more you assert your truth the less that the truth of other's can be perceived or related to. People start seeming unreal, language becomes too abstract to comprehend, and you become isolated in your own truth.

In order to cast any mystic spells you must first experience and form your own truths. The waves at a beach could impress upon you the meaning of life, a fallout with a friend could show you the cruelty of others, or a horrific illness could show you how fragile we all are. Mystics generally wandering the world, experiencing as much as they can. All of someone's truth form their inner world, the mechanism by which they perceive reality with it, and as they assert it more and more, the more it leaks into Reality and stops becoming just an "inner world". Perhaps your inner world is represented by a single, large mountain that all other truths are lesser peaks around. Eventually, this mountain would emerge from you, and you would become the mountain, and the mountain would become you.

Spells are all assertations of these truths, such as:
- "Your pain is not real", heal another's wounds
- "I cannot be touched by someone as weak as you", become immune to someone's attacks
- "Where darkness touches, nothing exists", cast something in shadow, it is gone

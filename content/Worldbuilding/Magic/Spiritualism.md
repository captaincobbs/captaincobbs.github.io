## Theology
No matter where you go in the [[World]], every mainstream culture and religion can agree on the existence of the quint-essences and what they each are-- this is undisputed among those of power in the civilized world. However, what they each mean and how they are interpreted varies wildly depending on your religious and cultural inclinations.

Now, what *is* the quintessence, and why does it matter? Well, frankly speaking, *you* are a quintessence. You are formed from all five essences, and without them you would be fundamentally lacking in some crucial way. When one refers to a "quintessence", they mean a vessel comprising all five essences, or generally speaking, to all five essences in isolation. Each essence is a fundamental aspect of a living being, and each are tangible and manipulable materials, even if some may seem abstract. The essences *are* what makes you, you, and are bestowed upon us from birth.
### Essences
- **Mind** - How you comprehend and model reality. It gives you the ability to reason, learn, and and understand.
- **Body** - How you interact with and manipulate the reality. Your physical form-- flesh, blood, organs-- the medium through which you act.
- **Spirit** - How you feel and resonate with the world. It is your emotions, instincts, drives, and the force that lets you connect to experiences on a visceral level.
- **Soul** - How you are defined and connected. It is your identity across time, your relationships to others, and the principles that guide your life.
- **Will** - How you choose and direct yourself forward. It is your intent, determination, and the force that lets you act with purpose and impose your desires onto the future.
#### Without a..
- **Mind**, you cannot understand, learn, or make sense of the world. You may act, but without comprehension or reasoning.
- **Body**, you cannot act upon the world. You may think or feel, but you have no means to affect reality.
- **Spirit**, you cannot feel, desire, or resonate. You exist without passion, instinct, or motivation-- completely empty of drive.
- **Soul**, you are unanchored and undefined. You have no lasting identity, no meaningful bonds, and no place within the greater world.
- **Will**, you cannot choose or pursue. You may think, feel, and exist, but you lack direction or purpose-- unable to commit, decide, or shape what comes next.
